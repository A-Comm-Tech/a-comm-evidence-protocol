Open evidence standard · Draft v1.0.3-rc.2 · Apache 2.0

When a consumer agent buys from your store, you cannot see who it was, what it was authorized to do, or whether the interaction was tampered with.

AEP records each step of an agent-mediated purchase as a typed artifact. Each artifact is hashed into the next. The chain is signed at authorization. Anyone holding the public key can verify it.

How it works

Canonicalize. Link. Seal. Verify.

Canonicalize

Every artifact is serialized with RFC 8785 JSON Canonicalization, so the same data always hashes the same way.

Link

Each artifact's SHA-256 hash includes the previous hash. Change one, and the rest no longer match.

Seal

At authorization the chain is signed with Ed25519. Fulfillment and refunds append after the seal.

Verify and export

A verifier recomputes the chain. A DisputeBundle packages the evidence for a dispute process.

Governance

Who is in charge. Who is not.

Today

  • The specification is edited by a maintainer group. During the bootstrap phase it is stewarded by A-Comm Technologies, Inc.
  • Changes go through public review. Every comment in a comment round gets a public disposition.
  • The published path: external maintainers, a published charter, then standards-body liaison.
  • A multi-stakeholder consortium is proposed. It is not yet formed.

Not in charge

  • AEP does not decide disputes. Networks, issuers and their rules do.
  • AEP does not assign liability. Evidence tiers describe verifiability only.
  • No company's product is required to implement AEP.
  • Implementing the specification needs no registration, fee or permission.

Scope

What the standard covers.

In the specification

  • Typed artifacts for each lifecycle segment
  • Hash chain, seal, post-seal appends
  • Signal classification: deterministic, attested, inferred, asserted
  • Two-plane privacy model and the Evidence Custodian role
  • External anchoring of sealed chains
  • DisputeBundle export and test vectors

Outside the specification

Optional block. Commercial services built on AEP are not part of the standard. If any are shown, each is labelled as a separate offering of a named company.
