# Identity, Biometrics & Delegation — Charter

**Status:** Provisional (pending member vote)

## Scope
Defining machine-readable consent structures, biometric authorization bindings, and credential attestation envelopes.

## Out of scope
Storage and isolation of credentials (tokenization-privacy). No biometric template or raw credential data is ever in scope for evidence artifacts.

## Outputs
Drafts for consent, authorization-binding, and attestation artifacts, with example payloads.

## Relationship to the spec
New artifact types and sub-wrappers are additive, so MINOR, and follow the proposal lifecycle in the working-groups README.
