# Tokenization, Privacy & Vaulting — Charter

**Status:** Provisional (pending member vote)

## Scope
Developing integration standards for secure enclaves, PCI tokenization vaults, and credential isolation layers.

## Out of scope
Identity and consent semantics (identity-delegation). Naming or ranking vault providers is out of scope under the neutrality rules.

## Outputs
Integration-standard drafts, privacy guidance, and verification rules for isolation boundaries.

## Relationship to the spec
Proposes through the normal MINOR path. May publish versioned profiles under `profiles/` once chartered, subject to confirming that spec §0.2 allows independent profile versioning.
